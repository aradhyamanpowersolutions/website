# Image generation brief — Aradhya Manpower Supplier

Aradhya Manpower Supplier is a labour contractor in the **Pithampur–Indore industrial
belt of Madhya Pradesh, India**. They supply workers into pharmaceutical and
manufacturing plants — clients include Cipla, Lupin, Ipca and Ajanta Pharma.

Every image below replaces a stock-looking placeholder currently on the site. The
brief is **authentic, professional, documentary photography of Indian industrial
workplaces** — real plants, real Indian workers, real PPE. Not glossy stock, not
Western factories with Indian faces pasted in.

## Instructions for the generating agent

1. Generate **20 images**, one per prompt below.
2. Save them into a single folder named `new-images/`.
3. Use the **exact filename** given in each prompt heading. Filenames matter — they
   get wired into the site by path.
4. Format: **JPEG, quality 85–90**. No transparency needed.
5. Use the **exact pixel dimensions** listed per image.
6. **Append the STYLE block and the NEGATIVE block (below) to every prompt**, verbatim.
7. Do not generate company logos, and do not put any brand name into any image.

---

## STYLE — append to every prompt

> Photorealistic editorial documentary photograph. Shot on a full-frame camera with a
> 35mm or 50mm prime lens, f/2.8–f/4, ISO 400. Natural available light mixed with the
> existing overhead industrial fixtures (LED battens, high-bay lamps); soft, believable
> shadows. True-to-life colour, neutral white balance, no HDR, no heavy contrast grade,
> no teal-and-orange look. Fine natural film grain, natural skin texture with visible
> pores and stubble, slight sensor noise in shadows. Everyone in frame is Indian (South
> Asian), ages 22–50, realistic non-model body types, natural expressions. Candid
> working moments — people mid-task, absorbed in the work, not posing for the camera and
> not smiling at the lens. Clothing and equipment show honest, light wear: faint scuffs,
> a crease, a smudge. The setting is an Indian industrial facility in Madhya Pradesh.

## NEGATIVE — append to every prompt

> No legible text anywhere — any signage, label, badge or screen must be out of focus,
> turned away, partially cropped, or blank. No logos or brand marks of any kind. No
> Caucasian, African or East Asian faces. No stock-photo clichés: no handshakes, no
> thumbs-up, no arms-crossed hero poses, no group smiling at camera, no pointing at a
> clipboard together. No illustration, cartoon, 3D render, CGI, or glossy AI look. No
> malformed or extra fingers, no duplicated limbs, no warped faces. No floating or
> physically impossible objects. No watermarks, borders, captions or frames. No lens
> flare or bokeh balls. No pristine showroom-clean surfaces — this is a working plant.

# 1 · Hero slideshow — 6 images

Wide plates across the top of the home page. **2400 × 1350 px (16:9).**

> ⚠️ **Safe area:** on desktop these are cropped to a 16:6 letterbox from the centre.
> Keep all faces and key action inside the **middle 60% horizontal band**. Leave the top
> and bottom fifth of the frame as floor, ceiling or machinery.

### `hero-01.jpg` — 2400 × 1350
A shift-change briefing just inside a factory gate in Pithampur, early morning. A
supervisor in a short-sleeved shirt and ID lanyard stands facing a loose semicircle of
nine or ten workers in navy-blue cotton coveralls and orange hi-vis vests, some in white
hard hats, several holding cotton work gloves. Warm low-angle sunlight rakes across them
from the left, long shadows on the concrete apron. A steel gate and painted kerb stripes
behind. Shot slightly wide from the side, capturing the whole group.

### `hero-02.jpg` — 2400 × 1350
A pharmaceutical gowning airlock. Four workers in the process of gowning up: white
one-piece cleanroom coveralls, blue hairnets, face masks pulled up, blue shoe covers,
nitrile gloves. One is pulling a hood over their hair, another seated on a low SS bench
fitting overshoes. Stainless steel lockers, a pass-through hatch, and a wall-mounted
sanitiser dispenser. Cool even fluorescent light, epoxy floor with a painted crossover
line. Clean, procedural, unhurried.

### `hero-03.jpg` — 2400 × 1350
A wide view down a pharmaceutical secondary-packing hall. A long stainless conveyor runs
away from the camera; eight to ten workers in white coats, blue hairnets and masks stand
along both sides, feeding blister strips into folding cartons and stacking finished
cartons into shippers. Overhead LED battens, pale green epoxy floor, cardboard shipper
stacks on a pallet at the near right. Busy, orderly, shot from chest height at the head
of the line.

### `hero-04.jpg` — 2400 × 1350
A finished-goods warehouse dispatch bay. Tall blue pallet racking stacked with shrink-
wrapped cartons recedes to the right. In the centre, four loaders in orange hi-vis vests
and safety shoes move cartons from a pallet onto a hand trolley toward an open truck
dock; a small forklift with an amber beacon waits at the left. Dust motes in a shaft of
daylight from the open shutter. Shot wide at waist height.

### `hero-05.jpg` — 2400 × 1350
A planned maintenance shutdown on a production floor. Three technicians in navy coveralls
and white hard hats work around a partly dismantled tablet-compression machine: one
kneeling with an open steel toolbox and a torque wrench, one holding a torch into an
opened guard panel, a third noting something on a clipboard turned away from camera.
Machine guards leaned against a pillar, a coil of yellow hose. Mixed overhead light and a
work lamp.

### `hero-06.jpg` — 2400 × 1350
End of shift outside a plant administration block. A group of about twelve workers and
two supervisors walk toward the camera across a paved yard, helmets under arms or hanging
from hands, lanyards swinging, talking in twos and threes. Low golden late-afternoon
light behind them. A cycle stand and a painted boundary wall out of focus in the
background. Relaxed, unposed, walking pace.

# 2 · Role photographs — 12 images

Used on `/services` as card images and on the home page as roster thumbnails.
**1600 × 1200 px (4:3).** The role must be unmistakable from the image alone.

### `maintenance-technicians.jpg` — 1600 × 1200
A maintenance technician in navy coveralls, white hard hat and safety glasses crouches at
the open side panel of a stainless-steel process machine, seating a drive belt with both
hands. An open steel toolbox and a vibration meter rest on the epoxy floor beside him.
Overhead high-bay light, a second technician blurred in the background. Three-quarter
view from low.

### `lab-assistants.jpg` — 1600 × 1200
A quality-control laboratory. A young woman lab assistant in a white lab coat, blue
hairnet, safety glasses and nitrile gloves stands at a bench transferring liquid with a
micropipette into a rack of clear vials. An HPLC unit and a fume hood behind her, a
notebook open beside the rack. Cool, even lab lighting; shallow depth of field on her
hands.

### `specialized-operators.jpg` — 1600 × 1200
A machine operator in white coat, hairnet and mask at the HMI control panel of a blister
packing machine, one hand on the panel, eyes on the running foil web. Formed blister
strips visible travelling through the machine to his right. Stainless steel and safety
yellow guarding, reflections of overhead LEDs on the panel glass. Shot over his shoulder
at a slight angle.

### `packing-staff.jpg` — 1600 × 1200
Three women in white coats, blue hairnets and masks work along a stainless packing table,
folding cartons and inserting printed leaflets, finished cartons stacked in a neat block
to one side. A shipper box waits open at the end of the table. Bright even overhead light,
pale epoxy floor. Shot at table height, hands and cartons sharp in the foreground.

### `loading-unloading-crew.jpg` — 1600 × 1200
Two loaders in orange hi-vis vests, cotton gloves and safety shoes pass a heavy carton
between them from a hand trolley into the back of a parked truck at a loading dock. A
third steadies the stack inside the truck. Daylight floods in from the open shutter
behind them; corrugated shipper boxes on a wooden pallet in the foreground. Shot from the
dock floor looking slightly up.

### `warehouse-workers.jpg` — 1600 × 1200
A warehouse worker in an orange hi-vis vest holds a handheld barcode scanner up to a
carton on the second level of blue pallet racking, reading a label turned away from the
camera. Rows of shrink-wrapped pallets recede behind him. Cool overhead warehouse light,
concrete floor with painted aisle lines. Shot from the aisle, racking leading the eye back.

### `office-assistants.jpg` — 1600 × 1200
A plant administrative office. A man in a plain formal shirt sits at a laminate desk
placing a document into a ring binder, a stack of files and a landline phone beside him.
A grey steel almirah and a wall-mounted whiteboard behind, tube-light overhead mixed with
window daylight. Modest, real Indian office — not a corporate showroom. Shot at desk height.

### `clerks.jpg` — 1600 × 1200
A records clerk in a plain shirt at a desk, one hand flattening a large bound attendance
register, the other on a computer keyboard, checking one against the other. Stacks of
older registers and a box file at the edge of the desk. Warm tube light, a window with a
grille out of focus behind. Shot slightly over the shoulder onto the register.

### `receptionist.jpg` — 1600 × 1200
A young woman receptionist in a simple formal kurta stands behind a plant reception
counter, handing a visitor gate pass and a lanyard across to a hand entering from frame
right. A visitor register lies open on the counter, a landline phone and a small potted
plant beside it. Daylight from a glazed entrance behind her. Warm, professional, calm.

### `project-coordinators.jpg` — 1600 × 1200
A project coordinator in a white hard hat and hi-vis vest over formal clothes stands on a
production floor holding a rolled drawing and a tablet, mid-conversation with two
technicians in coveralls; one gestures toward a machine off-frame. Plant machinery and a
mezzanine walkway behind. Mixed overhead and daylight. Shot at eye level, all three in frame.

### `temporary-technical-staff.jpg` — 1600 × 1200
A contract technician on a short-term shutdown job, kneeling at an opened electrical
control panel with the door swung wide, using a multimeter probe on a terminal block.
Neat looms of coloured wiring inside the panel. Hard hat, safety glasses, insulated
gloves. A work lamp throws hard light into the cabinet against the dim plant behind.

### `seasonal-workers.jpg` — 1600 × 1200
A peak-season overflow packing area. Six extra workers in white coats and blue hairnets
work at two folding tables assembling and taping shipper cartons, with a visibly larger
stack of flat-packed cartons waiting at the side. Slightly more crowded and improvised
than the main line — a stacked pallet, a floor fan. Bright overhead light. Shot wide to
show the volume of work.
